Privacy

Privacy notice.

Last updated November 2026

This notice explains how we use the details you give us through this website. We keep it simple. We only use your details to reply to you and to look after your booking.

Who we are

The Red Cow, The Green, Chesterton, Oxfordshire OX26 1UU, run by Danni & Alister. We are the controller of the personal data you send us through this website.

To ask anything about your data, email chestertonredcow@gmail.com or call 01869 932495.

What we collect and why

When you use the booking and enquiry form, we collect:

  • your first and last name
  • your email address
  • your phone number, if you give it
  • the date and party size, if you are booking
  • anything you write in your message

We use this to reply to you, to take and manage table bookings and private hire, and to contact you about your booking. We do not use it for marketing and we do not sell it.

If you email or phone us, we use what you tell us in the same way.

Our lawful basis

When you ask to book a table or hire the pub, we use your details to take steps you have asked for before an agreement with you. For general questions, we rely on our legitimate interest in answering messages from people who contact us.

Who receives your details

We use a small number of services to run this website and receive your messages:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co, based in the United States) passes the form to our inbox.
  • Google provides our Gmail inbox, where your message is stored.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States) hosts this website. Like any web host, it receives your IP address when you visit.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on this website. Your browser asks Google for them, so Google receives your IP address.
  • Google Maps loads only if you press "Show map".

We do not share your details with anyone else unless the law requires it.

International transfers

Some of these services are based in the United States, so your details may be processed there. These providers use safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK-US data bridge, where they apply.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking messages for up to 2 years, then delete them. If we need to keep something longer, for example for an ongoing private hire, we keep it only as long as we need it.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the details we hold about you
  • ask us to correct them
  • ask us to delete them
  • object to how we use them
  • ask us to limit how we use them
  • ask for them in a format you can take elsewhere

To use any of these rights, email or call us using the details above. We will reply within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we use your details, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address but does not set cookies through this website. The map is not loaded until you press "Show map". Google Maps may then set cookies. The Instagram and Facebook links simply open those sites, and they set their own cookies there.
